Building the Great Pyramid with different AI models

The model is asked to work out from first principles how the Great Pyramid could have been built with the technology of the time, and then to turn that building plan into an animated 3D website that shows the construction from the ground up.

The prompt:

Thinking from the first principles, think how a great pyramid could be build. Start from the assumptions that suitable stones are already available somewhere close to the construction side. Don't refere to other sources how this could have been done. You can use your exsiting knowledge or external information to find out about the size of the pyramid and size of the stones used there. Think what would be the approach you would take, considering the technology available at time the pyramids were constructed. You can assume unlimited amount of people and construction materials to get the job done. Once you have come up with the building plan, then I want you to build a website that uses webgl or similar technology that shows as animation how this works. All the way from ground up to the full pyramids, trying to include also some stick figures or something to animate the progress.
